HR automation: modules

Overview

One HR system that sets up a new joiner's tools from a single form, removes a leaver's access in one confirmed action, and records every change. It connects to Google Workspace, Gemini, Claude, GitHub and Google Cloud.

Problem

HR grants and removes tool access by hand, one tool at a time. Joiners wait for access, leavers can keep paid seats or sign-ins after they leave, and there is no single record of who had what.
